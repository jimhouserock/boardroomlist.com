# BoardroomList

BoardroomList collects senior job postings, Manager up through CEO, from the career sites of the 500 largest US companies (the Fortune 500), refreshed daily.

## Browsing and filtering

Anyone can browse and filter the listings for free by level, keyword, and other criteria to see what senior roles are open.

## What membership unlocks

Free browsing shows a teaser view of each listing. A paid membership reveals the company name, the full job description, and a direct link to apply. Applying always routes through BoardroomList, which checks membership before sending the applicant to the actual job posting.

## Finding a role

The fastest path is to filter by seniority level or search by keyword, then upgrade to membership once a listing looks worth applying to.
